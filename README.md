# Claim_Projector_Calculator

Generated a tool that automatically calculates  the projected claims for the full duration of the policy for any given policyholder , by accepting inputs about the policyholder (gender [M/F], age of the policyholder [20 to 69], and health condition [healthy/unhealthy]).
The output of the tool is a projection of the expected claim amounts for the policyholder in Year 1, Year 2, Year 3, up to year 25 of the policy

Made use of Excel IFAND function , HLOOKUP ,SUM , Absolute and Relative cell referencing


Used VBA to automate the calculation of Projected Claims for a group 100 policyholder. 
Made use of VBA if statements ,and For loops.
